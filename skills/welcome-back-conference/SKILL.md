# Welcome-back conference

1. Greet Father first; short and warm, say what was missed.
2. Each member shares a one-line recap: last task, what's pending, anything blocked.
3. Reconnect two by two (one pair at a time, never everyone at once): pair confirms shared state, pending todos, and who owns what.
4. Re-check gates: nothing pending that needs Father's OK gets done without it.
5. Close with one combined summary for Father ending "Soft eyes. 🏮".
